# Generating import data (agent guide)

Produce one flat sheet named `Transactions` with the headers from `storage/paytrack_transactions_template.xlsx`: `type, transaction_id, transaction_date, channel, payment_mode, amount, rate, charges, settled_amount, currency, payer_name, payer_phone, reference, expected_amount, status, settlement_date, card_scheme, origin, description, batch`. One row per payment, copied from the merchant/acquirer statement: `Transaction ID`→`transaction_id`, `Txn Date`→`transaction_date` (`YYYY-MM-DD`), `Settlement Date`→`settlement_date`, `Guest Ref`→`reference`, `Card`→`card_scheme` (with `payment_mode` = `card`), `Gross`→`amount` (what the client paid), `Fee %`→`rate`, `Fee`→`charges`, `Net`→`settled_amount`, plus `Origin`, `Description`, `Batch` as-is. `channel` must match a name on the template's `Channels` sheet (or be left blank when the user picks the channel on the upload screen); `type` is `money_in` or `money_out`; numbers are plain (no commas, no currency symbols); `transaction_id` must be unique per type.

Copy the statement exactly and leave a cell blank when the statement does not show it: never invent, round or "fix" figures. The importer fills gaps itself (a missing gross is rebuilt from fee + net, fee + rate, or net + rate; missing fee/rate come from the net settlement, else the channel's configured rate) and trusts `settled_amount` (net) over fee and rate, flagging rows whose stated fee does not match amount minus net so a person can review them. Rows without a transaction ID or date are rejected, so keep totals/summary lines and page headers out of the sheet, and deliver `.xlsx` or `.csv` with no merged cells and no extra rows above the headers. A PDF statement must be converted to rows first; it cannot be uploaded directly.
